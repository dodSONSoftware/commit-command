## Remaining risks and unsupported cases

- Writes support local macOS/APFS and files refs with ordinary checksum-valid v2/v3
  indexes only. Split/sparse/unmerged/extended-flag/v4 indexes, conflicting same-branch linked checkouts, detached/bare or
  in-progress states, content filters/conversions/custom attributesFile settings, symlinked writable metadata and
  foreign locks are rejected. M1 read support remains broader.
- Executable hooks/signers are trusted user code, not sandboxed. Unknown frameworks,
  reference-transaction Execute, transitive script/interpreter/agent dependencies,
  deliberate same-user replacement/hard-link attacks and confidentiality through
  includes/alternates are outside the guarantee. Explicit bypass is available.
- SIGKILL and injected durable-phase failures do not prove power-loss/OS-crash or
  every individual failed-syscall behavior. Partial locks/reflogs and external edits
  can require manual reconciliation. Recovery never overwrites foreign state.
- Post-hook attempt uncertainty persists; recovery never replays hooks or commits.
  Unreferenced objects/temporary artifacts may remain after interruption.
- Non-UTF-8 filesystem writes, Linux/Windows writes, network/non-APFS filesystems,
  reftable, X.509/dynamic SSH/interactive/hardware-token signing remain unsupported
  or untested. No platform release or packaging acceptance is implied.
- Private operation records/artifacts/backups retain sensitive messages/patches/
  paths and public signer information. Raw helper output, credentials and plaintext
  approval tokens are not persisted. Automatic pruning/encryption is not implemented;
  configurable 90-day retention remains proposed, with unresolved evidence protected.

## Next authorization boundary

M2 provides stable Core contracts for an M3 proposal. No M3 adapter, desktop
scaffolding, watcher, bulk executor, remote operation, AI or Action has been added.
Before enabling writes through a desktop adapter, it must render exact Core reviews,
require user gestures, preserve capability/session boundaries, surface uncertain/
published/post-hook states, and pass its own acceptance/security tests. Distribution,
UI accessibility/OS minimums and broader hook/signing/platform coverage remain open
product/release questions. Stop here and await user instructions; do not commit/push.
